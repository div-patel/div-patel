## About me

I build and run enterprise IT operations at scale. My work sits at the intersection of identity security, cloud infrastructure, automation, and AI adoption — with a focus on making complex systems simple to operate and govern.

Over 15 years across global SaaS businesses, I've built IT functions from scratch, integrated acquisitions, achieved ISO 27001 certification, and deployed enterprise AI platforms across distributed teams. I care about eliminating manual toil, enforcing least-privilege at scale, and ensuring the tools people use are adopted properly — not just provisioned.
